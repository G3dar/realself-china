Origin

Where Art Meets Breakthrough Technology

Real Self was born from the conviction that immersive experiences should not be something you watch — they should be something you become.

Backed by over twenty years of pioneering work in VR, mixed reality, and animation, the team behind Real Self brings together award-winning storytelling, deep technical expertise, and a relentless drive to create human experiences that leave a lasting mark.

Creator & Producer

Javier Drucaroff

Creator and producer of Real Self. With a multidisciplinary background spanning electronics, architecture, and theatrical production, Drucaroff is a pioneer in blending technology with participatory human experiences.

His vision for Real Self — an experience where projection mapping, surround sound, and guided narrative dissolve the boundary between spectator and performer — has been partnered with Sony and toured internationally.

CEO

German Heller

Emmy-nominated director and CEO of 3DAR and Eggscape Entertainment. Buenos Aires-born, Heller founded 3DAR in 2004 at age 23, building it into one of Latin America's most innovative studios.

His projects — including Paper Birds with Edward Norton, Gloomy Eyes narrated by Colin Farrell, and the Venice Prize-winning Eggscape — have screened at Sundance, Venice, Tribeca, and beyond.
